If the fire alarm sounds

  1. Leave the building straight away by the nearest fire exit.
  2. Don't use the lifts, and don't stop to collect belongings.
  3. Go to the fire assembly point and tell your host or a fire marshal that you're out.
  4. Don't go back in until you're told it's safe.
