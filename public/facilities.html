Amenities

Level 0 and cycle shed

Showers and drying areas

8 showers, 3 of them accessible: inside the building by the Level 0 toilets, and in the external shower block in the larger cycle shed. Fresh towels in every shower.

Drying areas for wet coats and towels are in the shower block and beside the Level 0 showers. The shower block needs a fob from Workplace Experience.

Level 0 and Level 1

Lockers

More than 100 lockers for daily use, on Level 1 and by the Level 0 hydration point and copy area. Need a permanent locker? Contact Workplace Experience.

Cyclists: 45 more lockers in the larger cycle shed, for semi-permanent storage.

Level 0 and Level 1

Print areas

A print area on each floor. For help, visit the IT Hub on Level 0.

Every floor

Drink stations and kitchens

Self-service tea, coffee, fresh juice, squash and fresh fruit. Every station has fridges, crockery and cutlery, and there are microwaves in the Level 0 kitchens.

Level 0, by the restaurant

Games area

A pool table and board games.
