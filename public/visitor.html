Construction work on site

Phase Two is being built next to us. Follow the safety signs, keep out of construction zones, use the marked vehicle and pedestrian routes, and stay on the pavements and walkways.

If the fire alarm sounds

  1. Leave the building straight away by the nearest marked exit.
  2. Don't stop to collect your belongings.
  3. Follow the signs to the fire assembly point: Level 0, multi-storey car park.

Hosting guests

Questions? Email Workplace Experience

External guests

Verkada
  1. Pre-register your guest in My Verkada (opens in a new tab). They get an email with a link to register.
  2. Your guest registers and receives a confirmation with our address and your details.
  3. When they arrive, they sign in at the reception kiosk and you get an email.
  4. Meet your guest at reception and stay with them throughout their visit.
  5. At the end, walk them back to reception and make sure they return their visitor pass.

Colleagues from other offices

  1. Let Workplace Experience know they're coming.
  2. Check desk availability with your Cambridge colleagues or Workplace Experience.
  3. On the day, they check in at reception for a visitor pass.

They won't get an access card, but the visitor pass lets them move freely around the site once they're through the barriers.
